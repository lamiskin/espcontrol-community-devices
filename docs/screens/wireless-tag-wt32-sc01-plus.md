# 3.5-inch Wireless-Tag WT32-SC01 Plus

The **Wireless-Tag WT32-SC01 Plus** is a 3.5-inch landscape
touchscreen powered by **ESP32-S3**, with room for **6 cards**
on the home screen.

Community port contributed via [PR #194](https://github.com/jtenniswood/espcontrol/pull/194) by @jonnybergdahl.
